Paleta

Cut no. 3 · Argentine pork · Picnic shoulder

The shoulder is the front quarter, several working muscles with plenty of collagen. It wants a low oven or a long braise, and it is the classic meat for grinding.

How to cook it

Slow-roasted shoulder

Oven · 240 min · Oven at 160 °C, covered

Done when: Falling apart, 90 °C

  1. The day before, pierce the piece and season with garlic, salt, lemon, onion and herbs.
  2. Roast covered at 160 °C for about 3 hours, basting with the marinade.
  3. Uncover, raise to 200 °C and brown for 40 to 60 min.
  4. Rest 15 min and serve in chunks.

Pork meatballs

Burger · 30 min · Pan over medium heat, then sauce

Done when: 71 °C in the center

  1. Grind the meat with some of its fat and mix with milk-soaked bread, egg, garlic, parsley and salt.
  2. Roll walnut-sized balls and brown over medium heat.
  3. Finish 10 to 15 min in tomato sauce, until 71 °C in the center.
