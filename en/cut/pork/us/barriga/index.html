Belly

Cut no. 12 · American pork

Belly is layers of fat and meat under the skin. Slow cooking makes it tender, and high heat turns the skin into crackling.

How to cook it

Crackling pork belly

Oven · 150 min · Oven at 160 °C, then 240 °C

Done when: Puffed, crackling skin

  1. The day before, season the meat side, dry the skin, cover it in coarse salt and refrigerate.
  2. Brush off the salt and roast skin side up at 160 °C for 2 hours.
  3. Raise to 240 °C and roast 20 to 30 min more, until the skin pops.
  4. Rest 10 min and cut into cubes.

Braised pork belly

Braise · 150 min · Low heat, covered pot

Done when: Fork tender

  1. Cut into large cubes and brown on every side in a heavy pot.
  2. Add onion, garlic, ginger, a little sugar and soy sauce, and cover with water.
  3. Simmer covered over low heat for 2 hours.
  4. Uncover and reduce the liquid to a glaze over the meat.
