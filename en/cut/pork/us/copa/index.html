Boston Butt

Cut no. 1 · American pork · Collar (shoulder butt)

The collar sits high on the neck and shoulder. It is well marbled, which keeps it juicy both as steaks over coals and in long, slow cooking.

How to cook it

Grilled collar steaks

BBQ · 20 min · Medium coals

Done when: Pull at 63 °C

  1. Cut 2 cm steaks and season with salt, garlic and a little lemon.
  2. Grill 5 to 6 min per side, turning once.
  3. Check for 63 °C in the center and rest 3 min before serving.

Oven pulled pork

Oven · 360 min · Low oven, 140 °C, covered

Done when: Pulls apart, 90–95 °C

  1. The day before, rub the whole piece with salt, brown sugar, paprika and garlic and refrigerate.
  2. Roast covered with foil at 140 °C for about 5 hours.
  3. Uncover and roast 1 more hour, until 90–95 °C in the center.
  4. Rest 20 min, pull apart with two forks and stir in the pan juices.
