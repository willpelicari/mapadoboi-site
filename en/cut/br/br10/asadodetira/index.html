Ponta de agulha (no. 10) · Brazilian

Short ribs sawn across the bones into thin strips (flanken cut). Richly marbled meat around small round bone sections, with deep rib flavor. Quick and easy to grill.

In other countries

  • Argentina: Tapa de asado, Asado, Falda, Matambre, Entraña
    The ponta de agulha is the lower ribs and the belly flap in one piece, with the matambre on top and the diaphragm inside. Argentina cuts that area in five: asado (the ribs cut across), falda (the lower chest), tapa de asado (the muscle sheet over the ribs), matambre (the thin skin muscle) and entraña (the diaphragm). Each is a part of it.
  • United States: Short ribs, Skirt steak
    The US cuts this area as the short plate: short ribs from the rib bones and skirt steak from the diaphragm. The ponta de agulha holds both, along with meat the US trims or grinds, so each is a part of it.
  • Portugal: Aba das costelas, Prego do peito, Aba delgada
    The ponta de agulha is the lower ribs and the belly in one piece, from the 6th rib back. Portugal splits that area: the aba das costelas (the middle of ribs 1 to 10), the prego do peito (their lower end, with the breast) and the aba delgada (behind the 10th rib). Each holds part of the ponta de agulha.

How to cook it

Grilled short rib strips

BBQ · 10 min · Strong embers, grate at 15 cm

Done when: Pull at 54 °C center

  1. Choose strips of even thickness, 1.5 to 2 cm.
  2. Sprinkle crushed coarse salt on both sides.
  3. Grill 4 minutes on the first side and 3 to 4 minutes on the second.
  4. Pull at 54 °C, rest 2 minutes, and serve whole or cut between the bones.

Korean-style marinated

Pan · 8 min · Strong embers or very hot skillet

Done when: Pull at 54 °C center

  1. Marinate the strips in soy sauce, garlic, sesame oil, and sugar for 2 hours.
  2. Lift the strips out of the marinade and drain well.
  3. Grill 3 minutes per side until caramelized.
  4. Pull at 54 °C and serve hot with rice or lettuce wraps.

Thick strips over medium embers

BBQ · 20 min · Medium embers, grate at 30 cm

Done when: Pull at 54 °C center

  1. Use thicker strips, about 3 cm thick.
  2. Coat the surface with coarse salt 10 minutes ahead.
  3. Grill 30 cm above the embers for 10 minutes on the first side and 8 minutes on the second.
  4. Pull at 54 °C and tap off the excess salt before serving.
