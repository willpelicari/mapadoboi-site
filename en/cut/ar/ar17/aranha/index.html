Aranha

Cuadril (no. 17) · Argentine

A trim of the top sirloin: the thin, flat meat lining the hip bone, where the top sirloin ends and the top round begins. Named for its veins of fat, spread like a spider's web. Tender and short-grained, good as a grilled steak, breaded, or braised.
