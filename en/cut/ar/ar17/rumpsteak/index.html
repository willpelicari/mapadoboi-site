Miolo da alcatra

Cuadril (no. 17) · Argentine

The centre of the top sirloin, trimmed of both the picanha and the tri-tip. A lean cut with short, tender fibers and a balanced beef flavor. Ideal for quick steaks, grilling, and skewers.

In other countries

  • Brazil: Alcatra
    Cuadril and Alcatra are the same cut: the heart of the rump once the cap and the tri-tip come off, mostly the gluteus medius. In Brazilian shops, alcatra can also mean the whole piece with the picanha and maminha still on.
  • United States: Top sirloin
    Cuadril and Top sirloin are the same cut: the heart of the rump once the cap and the tri-tip come off, mostly the gluteus medius.
  • Portugal: Cheio da alcatra
    Cuadril and Cheio da alcatra are the same cut: the heart of the rump once the cap and the tri-tip come off, mostly the gluteus medius. In Portugal the alcatra is the whole rump, with the folha and the ponta; the cheio is its heart, also sold as coração da alcatra.

How to cook it

Grilled rump steak

BBQ · 10 min · Strong embers, grate at 15 cm

Done when: Pull at 54 °C center

  1. Cut the centre into thick 3 cm steaks across the grain.
  2. Season both sides with crushed coarse salt.
  3. Grill 4 minutes on one side and 3 minutes on the other.
  4. Pull at 54 °C and rest 3 minutes before serving.

Skewered steak cubes

BBQ · 12 min · Strong embers, skewer at 20 cm

Done when: Pull at 54 °C center

  1. Cut the centre into 3.5 cm cubes.
  2. Thread the cubes onto a double skewer and sprinkle with coarse salt.
  3. Roast 20 cm above the embers for 10 minutes, turning often, until browned all over.
  4. Pull at 54 °C, tap off the excess salt, and serve right away.

Pan-seared in cast iron

Pan · 8 min · High heat, a little oil

Done when: Pull at 54 °C center

  1. Pat the 2.5 cm steaks dry with paper towels.
  2. Season with fine salt and ground black pepper.
  3. Sear in hot oil for 3 minutes on one side and 2 minutes on the other.
  4. Pull at 54 °C and rest 2 minutes before slicing.
